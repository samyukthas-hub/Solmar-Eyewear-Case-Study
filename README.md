# Solmar Eyewear — Revenue & Marketing Performance Analysis
**Lifesight | Round 2 Case Study**

**Author:** **Samyuktha S | Christ(Deemed to be University)**


## Overview

A Tableau-based business analysis of **Solmar Eyewear's weekly revenue, marketing spend, customer revenue, and promotional activity** across 109 weeks from August 2024 to August 2026.

The objective was to identify key revenue patterns, understand the relationship between media investment and revenue, evaluate promotional periods, and derive actionable business insights.

## Key Findings

- Total Revenue: **$93.95M**
- Total Paid Media Spend: **$18.65M**
- Aggregate Revenue / Media Spend: **5.04x**
- Repeat Customer Revenue: **$50.96M (54.2%)**
- New Customer Revenue: **$42.99M (45.8%)**
- BFCM average weekly revenue: **~$2.46M**
- Non-BFCM average weekly revenue: **~$0.80M**

## Analysis Performed

- Weekly revenue trend and seasonality analysis
- Revenue vs. media spend relationship
- New vs. repeat customer revenue analysis
- BFCM performance comparison
- Promotional activity analysis
- Marketing channel spend analysis
- Price-hike comparison

## Business Insights

- Revenue shows clear seasonal variation with significant peaks during major promotional periods.
- Media spend has a strong positive association with revenue; however, this does **not establish causality**.
- Repeat customers contribute a larger share of revenue than new customers.
- BFCM weeks show substantially higher average revenue than non-BFCM weeks.
- Marketing investment is concentrated across selected Meta and Google channels.
